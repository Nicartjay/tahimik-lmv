# Treatment

*Tahimik Pero Ako ’To* is filmed as a notebook: **field notes of a quiet person**. Each
plate is a page, `TALA NN · TITLE`, drawn in hairlines on a dark ground with the
vocabulary of a surveyor's sketchbook — dimension lines, callouts, registration marks,
rulers, a faint graph-paper grid. The song never asks the quiet one to change, so the
film doesn't either: it only measures them more kindly as it goes.

## Rules

- **One warm light.** The alitaptap (firefly) is the only warm, blooming thing in the film.
  It lives in the protagonist's chest. Its brightness is `LIWANAG`, a single curve over
  the song (`timeline.ts`) that stays at 0.02 until the first *“May sarili rin akong
  mundo”* and reaches 1.00 on the last *“ako”*. Everything else stays below the bloom
  threshold.
- **The margins until GITNA.** The self and the lyrics keep to the edges and thirds of the
  frame. The centre belongs to the crowd, the stage, the spotlight — until plate 18, the
  only centred composition in the film.
- **Two voices in type.** Fraunces (serif, soft optical size) for the inner voice and the
  sung lines; Archivo (grotesque) for the loud world — chatter, laughter, other people's
  words; IBM Plex Mono for the notebook's own labels and the HUD.
- **Sync, not decoration.** Lyrics are word- and letter-timed; motion lands on beats,
  bars and stem onsets from the song's analysis. Nothing moves at random.
- **The chorus repeats and changes.** DAMI NG TAO and SULOK return three times (`v1–v3`);
  the same drawing each time, with the self a little more present.

## HUD

The notebook frame: series title and `TALA NN · TITLE` top-left; `LAKAS` (vocal level,
dB) and `LIWANAG` meters bottom-left; `SUKAT` (bar.beat) and the timecode bottom-right.

## Plates

| # | plate | lines | |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00 | PABALAT | intro | The notebook cover. A faint grid, registration marks, the title set low on the left, never centred. One firefly wanders in, becomes the title's full stop, then slips into the chest of the quiet one. |
| 01 | GILID | *Nakatayo sa gilid lang…* | The self at the far-left end of a ground line; a group of six talks and sways centre-right. A dimension line measures the gap: `LAYO · 4 HAKBANG`. On *“sumasabay”* the group sways in unison — the self does not. |
| 02 | ALON | *Tawa nila parang alon / Ako ’yung bato sa tabing-baybayin* | The same ground as GILID; the gap between the self and the group becomes a cross-section of a sea basin. Their laughter launches a swell on each beat (loudness and drums set its height), and Archivo *“haha”* ride the crests. On *“bato”* the self becomes a stone — dashed outline, the chest light still inside — and the waves break against it. `AKO = BATO`, `TAWA · ALON`, a running `ALON · NN`. |
| 03 | LALAMUNAN | *May sagot na sa isipan / Pero ’di makalusot sa lalamunan* | The first paper plate: a pencil cross-section of the head, `FIG. 03`. The answer writes itself in the dashed mind; on *“makalusot”* its letters drop into the throat and pile up at a knot that tightens on the beats (`LALAMUNAN · Ø NN`, `SARADO`). On *“Nagpapaliban”* they climb back up and are boxed away as `MAMAYA NA`, with a tally for *“na naman”*. |
| 04 | DAMI NG TAO | *Nahihiya sa dami ng tao…* | A crowd in perspective fills the centre and right, bobbing on the beat; a pale, cool spotlight hunts through it. *“sentro”* marks the frame centre; on *“eksena”* the light swings to the quiet one at the left edge, who shrinks and slips back out of it. |
| 05 | SULOK | *Gusto ko lang huminga nang tahimik…* | A room corner in perspective hairlines, lower-left; the self sits in it, knees drawn up. Sea-coloured breath rings leave the chest on the bar; outside, the crowd's noise thins and drifts away. `KILALA · 1`. |
| 06 | SIMULA NOON | *Sadyang mahiyain lang talaga ako / Ganito na yata ’ko simula noon* | Paper. Tilted album prints with photo corners drop in on the beats — 2008 KINDER, 2010 KAARAWAN, 2012, 2014 — each with the young self at its edge. On *“ako”* every self is ringed in pencil, one per half-beat, and tagged `AKO`; captions lead down to a year ruler. *“Ganito”* types the tally `NASA GILID · n SA n LITRATO`; on *“simula noon”* a pencil arrow runs back along the ruler to a ringed `NOON`. |
| 07 | SARILING MUNDO | *Pero kahit ’di ako sumisigaw / May sarili rin akong mundo* | *“sumisigaw”* — a `SIGAW` level meter that reads 0.00. On *“May sarili rin akong mundo”* the camera leans into the chest light and a circle opens at the right, a detail view `A`: pencil ridges, a tiny seated self, fireflies that light on the beat as `LIWANAG` first rises. |
| 08 | BULSA | *May mga kwento… / Nakatupi sa bulsa ng pantalon / Gustong-gusto kong ilabas / Pero nanahimik na lang* | Paper. A pencil drawing of a jeans back pocket, lower-left, `FIG. 08 · BULSA NG PANTALON, KALIWA`, `LALIM · 16 CM`. From *“kwento”* seven folded notes are counted into it on the eighths (`KWENTO · n`, `NAKATUPI`). One note tugs twice on *“Gustong-gusto”*, rises on *“kong”* and half-opens on *“ilabas”* — handwriting: *yung aso naming si Bantay…*, *pangarap kong ★*, a crossed-out *maging*, *sana sinabi ko…* — then folds shut on *“nanahimik”* and is back in the pocket by *“lang”*. |
| 09 | ENTABLADO | *Pinapanood… / Sa entablado ng ating paaralan… / Ako nama’y nanonood lang / Nakatalikod, ngumingiti nang lihim* | A line-art school stage — curtains, an `ARAW NG PAARALAN` banner, `ENTABLADO · 12 M` — with three performers under cool spotlights that swell on *“sumikat”*, three rows of heads in the foreground. The self stands by the `LABASAN` door; a dashed `TINGIN` sightline runs to the stage. On *“Nakatalikod”* the self turns away and the sightline retracts; on *“ngumingiti”* a small smile, the chest light swells, and a callout names it `LIHIM`. |
| 10 | DAMI NG TAO · II | | A denser crowd; the light catches the self for a beat, then they slip away. |
| 11 | SULOK · II | | A fuller room, slower and wider breaths, a notebook on the floor. |
| 12 | SIMULA NOON · II | | The four prints slide into a row of eight over one bar, up to 2026 `NGAYON` — and in the newest one the self carries a gold chest mark. `NASA GILID · 8 SA 8 LITRATO`. |
| 13 | SARILING MUNDO · II | | A wider circle: a small lit house, a book, twenty fireflies, and a constellation drawn in the shape of the self. |
| 14 | ISANG HAKBANG | bridge | A callback to GILID: the same ground line, the same group, the same `LAYO · 4 HAKBANG`. *“lakas-loob”* swells the chest light and straightens the self; on *“paabante”* the self takes exactly one step (lift on the beat, land on the downbeat) and the note is corrected 4 → 3. *“dahan-dahan lang”* pencils in the next small steps, one per bar — not taken. | 15 | DAMI NG TAO · III | | After the bridge the light finds the self, and the self stays — taller, lit. |
| 16 | SULOK · III | | The firefly lights the corner: books, a plant, a picture — a room of one's own. |
| 17 | BUO | *Sadyang mahiyain lang talaga ako / At kung tanggapin ko ’to nang buo* | Six shards of earlier plates drift in around a dashed outline: a photo frame (SIMULA NOON), a wave (ALON), a throat (LALAMUNAN), a folded note (BULSA), a stage arch (ENTABLADO), a breath ring (SULOK). From *“tanggapin”*, one per beat, each bends into its part of the self; the ring becomes the head exactly on *“buo”*, the outline closes and fills, and the chest light flares. |
| 18 | GITNA | *Baka sa tahimik kong paraan / Mag-stand out din ako* | The only centred plate. The crowd owns the middle; the self walks in from the left margin, one step per beat, and the crowd parts and recedes to faint outlines. A registration mark labelled `GITNA` waits at frame centre; the camera settles the self's chest exactly on it as *“ako”* lands — the peak of the video. |
| 19 | WAKAS | outro | The notebook closes. The cover returns; the light from GITNA drifts down and becomes the title's full stop again. The grid goes, the type goes, and the full stop is the last light out. |
